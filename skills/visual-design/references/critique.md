# Art direction and a useful critique loop

A modern result comes from hierarchy, type, composition and content, not a prescribed palette or a large effects library. Follow the user's references and existing brand; this workflow leaves the visual style open.

## Before production

Inspect the real assets, audience and final viewing size. For an open brief, inspect a few relevant references from the product's industry or original designers. Describe what is useful about their composition, imagery, type or interaction; don't copy a page wholesale. Choose one coherent direction and a short token set. If the user already supplies a direction, use it rather than starting an unnecessary mood-board exercise.

Sketch two materially different compositions cheaply when direction is uncertain. Select one against the brief, then build a representative part with real copy and imagery. For a website, this is the hero plus one content-rich section and its phone layout. For a poster, it is the actual headline, subject and brand at delivery ratio. For slides, it is a title slide plus the densest slide. Do not build twenty near-identical screens before checking one.

## Inspect the rendered result, not just the source

Use the visible preview or export to view the real pixels. Check:

| Dimension | Concrete question |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Is the product and primary action understandable at a glance? |
| Composition | Does the eye follow one clear hierarchy? Are margins and optical alignments intentional? |
| Typography | Are headline breaks natural, body text readable, glyphs available and line lengths comfortable? |
| Assets | Are photographs/illustrations appropriate, consistent and sharp? Are the actual mascot and logo preserved? |
| Craft | Do controls align, long text wrap, and narrow layouts retain usable actions? |
| Interaction | Are empty/loading/error/focus/disabled states designed and functional? |
| Motion | Does movement explain something? Is it interruptible and reduced-motion friendly? |
| Delivery | Do size, crop, language and editable/export formats match the request? |

Identify the two or three largest defects and correct those before adding decoration. Re-render and compare the changed areas. Stop when the brief and quality checks are met; do not regenerate assets or fetch inspiration on every pass. Maintain the chosen direction unless evidence shows it fails the brief.

Use frontend-design for web art direction, interface-quality for interaction, webapp-testing for own-app verification, media-production for generated assets and slides for presentations. A code-only solution is not obligatory: existing design apps, SVG/layout tools and generation APIs each have a place. Select through tool availability, output quality and editable deliverables, not familiarity alone. Deliver the artifact with done.files.
