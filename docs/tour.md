## Chat and Studio

localDot has two views, and each device keeps its own choice. Studio shows the computer, the chat and
every step together. Chat is like texting dot, with the computer a tap away; a phone starts there.
Switch any time from the ⋯ menu, Settings → Appearance or ⌘K.

Chat opens on your conversations, whichever needs you first. Tap one: dot's replies are bubbles, and
"9:20 · 7 steps" under one unfolds what it did. On a phone, swipe right anywhere to go back; from tablet
width up the list sits beside the chat.

The foot of the chat is one dot and one sentence. While that task has the computer, a small live
picture sits beside them: tap it, or the screen button at the top, and it zooms into the computer,
with Pause, Stop, Take control and a box to steer dot. Drag down or swipe right to return.

Hold a reply for Copy, Select text, Show steps, Watch this part and Share. Approvals wait above the
message field until you answer, with everything dot would do on show. Prefer less dot? Tap it and
choose Hide dot; it tucks into ⋯, and Show dot there brings it back.
