## What's new

- Run several tasks at once and switch between them in the new list on the left, with each task following its own windows.
- Press ⌘K to find and run any action, or type / in the chat for commands like /btw, /resume and /rename.
- Conversations name themselves, Auto can pick the dot for a task, and a light theme joins a System option.
- The chat shows dot's steps and pictures inline, and files you attach or dot makes open in a viewer with their changes.
- Finished recordings are re-encoded in the background to take about a ninth of the space.

## Fixes

- A new conversation starts on a blank browser tab, and a finished task no longer keeps the browser from others.
- Previews open quickly, skip links that don't answer, and follow redirects and other ports.
- Clicking the screen while dot works no longer stops following it; scrolling or picking a step does.
- Buttons near panel edges, such as Revise, have room again.

## Before you update

- Your computer and web browser update once, and your files and sign-ins stay.
- Screenshots older than 180 days are removed, except each task's cover picture.
