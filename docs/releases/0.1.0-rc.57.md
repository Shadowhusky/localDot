## What's new

- Mark up a picture from its preview with a pen, an arrow or a box, then add the marked copy to your message.
- Studio's list of conversations shows your tags on one line, so you can show only the conversations with one tag.
- Clicking a notification from dot on your Mac brings your open localDot tab to the front at that task.
- A run of steps opens taller, so the pictures in it are easier to look over.

## Fixes

- Deleting or archiving a conversation no longer changes how the other conversations' dots look.
- localDot answers much sooner when you have many conversations, so connecting to it no longer stalls.
- Routines that Stop everything paused stay easy to continue after an update or a reload, with a notice in Routines.

## Before you update

- The first time you click a notification, macOS asks whether localDot may control your browser to bring its tab forward.
