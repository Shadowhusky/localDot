## What's new

- A message you send while dot is waiting now cuts the wait short, and a late Auto pick still applies before dot starts.
- On a phone, conversations under way show as live cards, and you can swipe a conversation to pin or archive it or hold it to preview it.
- Thinking and effort settings now cover every provider whose documentation offers them, including Kimi K3.1 Pro.
- After you switch to another localDot, it lists the one you came from, with its name and dot.

## Fixes

- A Claude Code or ChatGPT task can now start while another task is working.
- A reply cut off mid-stream is retried instead of pausing the task.
- The list on the left scrolls smoothly while magnified, and right-clicking anywhere on a conversation opens its menu.
- In full screen on a phone, the status pill no longer overlaps the buttons beside it.
- Your dot's accessory, plan usage in the model menu, button spacing and closing previews by clicking outside are fixed.
