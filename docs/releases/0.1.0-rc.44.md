## What's new

- When something only you can fix stops dot, like locked keys or an ended app sign-in, a card to fix it appears in the conversation.
- A message sent while dot waits on a question takes its place, and dot works on it straight away.
- /btw can now look through the task's log, files, screen, memory and the web, and asks the main model when the fast one can't find the answer.
- Talk to dot with speech turned into text on your own computer, using a model you choose in setup or Settings.
- Deleting a conversation pops its dot like a soap bubble.

## Fixes

- Updates show each stage with its progress, and offer Retry instead of looking stuck.
- "Connecting your screen" no longer stays up until you refresh.
- Accessories sit properly on the dot, and many were redrawn to be easier to recognise.
- Studio's chat panel drags much wider, opening a step no longer jumps the page, and empty scrollbars are gone.
- A shared link sees only its own tasks and the conversation you're showing, not every conversation.

## Before you update

- Updating from rc.43 shows the old update screen one last time.
- A task stopped by something you fix outside its own card now waits for you to press Continue.
