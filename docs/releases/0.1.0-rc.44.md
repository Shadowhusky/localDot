- When something only you can fix stops dot, like locked keys or an ended app sign-in, a card to fix it appears in the conversation.
- When your Claude or ChatGPT plan reaches its limit, dot carries the task on by itself once the plan resets.
- An overview shows every dot's screen and progress side by side.
- /btw can now look through the task's log, files, screen, memory and the web.
- Talk to dot with speech turned into text on your own computer.

## Fixes

- Updates show each stage with its progress, and offer Retry instead of looking stuck.
- Coming back to the app no longer says it lost touch with your Mac.
- With the dot hidden, what dot is doing shows above the message box, and long replies stay inside their bubble.
- "Connecting your screen" no longer stays up until you refresh.
- A shared link sees only its own tasks and the conversation you're showing, not every conversation.
