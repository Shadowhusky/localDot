## What's new

- Name your own tags, each with a colour, and give a conversation as many as fit. The colours still work as tags. Find, make, rename or delete them from "Tags…" in a conversation's menu or its details, and show only the conversations with one from the list.
- Sort into tags: your model reads your conversations and tags each, as broadly or finely as you choose, with your tags or new ones. It only adds tags, and Undo takes the whole sort back. Switch on tagging new conversations as they finish to keep the list sorted.

## Fixes

- A conversation's More sits at the list's edge, its date beside it, where a mouse can reach it.

## Before you update

- Each colour you gave a conversation before stays as that colour's tag.
