## What's new

- Name your own tags, each with a colour, give a conversation as many as fit, and show only the conversations with one from the list.
- Sort into tags has your model read your conversations and tag each, as broadly or finely as you choose, and its Undo takes the whole sort back.
- New conversations can be tagged as they finish, from a switch in Sort into tags.

## Fixes

- A conversation's More sits at the list's edge, its date beside it, where a mouse can reach it.

## Before you update

- Each colour you gave a conversation before stays as that colour's tag.
