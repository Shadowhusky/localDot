## What's new

- The model menu shows how much of your Claude or ChatGPT plan's five-hour and weekly limits you have used.
- When your saved keys are locked, you can unlock them right in the chat or in Settings, without a terminal.
- Report a problem from Settings or with localdot report, and only a summary with private details removed is sent.
- Replay can free up space in one step, by age or by the recordings you select.
- You can run more than one dot on a computer, each with its own keys, sign-ins and computer.

## Fixes

- The web browser recovers by itself after a crash or a restart, and dot is told exactly why it isn't ready yet.
- Claude Code tasks no longer stop straight away when you have connected apps.
- A task that ends with a problem no longer turns your computer off, and restarting localDot no longer leaves it frozen.
- Helpers dot started carry on where they stopped after a usage limit or an error.
- The screen keeps its last picture while it reconnects, and steps in the chat open in place.

## Before you update

- The web browser restarts once; your sign-ins stay.
