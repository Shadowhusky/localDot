## What's new

- The app speaks Simplified Chinese: choose it in Settings → Appearance → Language, or let it follow your device.
- The web browser is the one browser on dot's desktop: the desktop's own Chromium, kept for sites dot builds on the computer, has left the dock and menus.

## Fixes

- While the browser restarts for a sign-in without dot's connection, the screen says so instead of the window just vanishing.
- dot finishes a sign-in itself where it can, with a saved login or a code you read out, and hands the computer over only for what needs your hands.

## Before you update

- The dock change arrives with each computer's next update; until then its dock keeps the desktop's Chromium.
