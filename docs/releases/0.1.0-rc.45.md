## Fixes

- After Replace computer, the old computer's home no longer stays behind taking up space once the new one is ready, and one an earlier replacement left goes when localDot next starts.
- What you type while dot waits for your passphrase stays in the message box after you unlock, unless it holds the passphrase itself.
- Clicking a localDot banner on your Mac opens localDot at the task it is about.
- A shared link's conversation no longer turns empty when a task joins it some other way, as at a plan's limit, and a guest's choice of thinking level now applies.
- The Conversations title on a phone no longer runs under its buttons, and the line under a task's circle lines up with its steps.
