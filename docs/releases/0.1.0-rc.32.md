## What's new

- Updates show a progress bar and what's new, and installing one only restarts localDot.
- Choose Fast or Ultrafast speed where your provider offers it, and a fast model from the same provider.
- Queued messages stay at the bottom of the conversation, and Edit puts one back in the input.
- dot's questions always offer "Other…", and you can leave optional ones to dot with "Let dot decide".
- New animations show dot running commands, reading, searching, planning, remembering, scheduling and working with helpers.

## Fixes

- The conversation no longer jumps back while you scroll as dot writes.
- The blue border stays on for as long as dot works.
- Helpers no longer look busy forever after they finish.
- Settings explains Tailscale's status, with the next step and a link to allow HTTPS.
- Adding a localDot from a remote link works.

## Before you update

- Your computer restarts once; your files and sign-ins stay.
